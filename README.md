# THE DAILY ATLAS — Friday 20 November 2026

A one-page picture of the world's news on Friday 20 November 2026. The page carries 40 verified Friday stories: 15 illustrated cards lead the layout — each with a painted scene and a source status label — a BY THE NUMBERS strip pulls out the day's key figures (GTA 6's $2.36bn day-one sales, Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO, the Nikkei's −5.1% Friday close, the ICJ's 9–6 genocide ruling against Myanmar, and the 110 missiles in Russia's Friday attack on Ukraine), a THE DAY IN UTC timeline follows six key moments of the day, and a DATE CHECK strip states that every story happened or was first reported on Friday 20 November 2026. A dotted world map below carries a numbered pin for every story, so each item on the page can be located at a glance, next to a 25-item ALSO ON FRIDAY list. The day's biggest developments are all here: the first-ever Xi–Trump–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen and the Peace Declaration they issued, the UK–EU summit agreeing three deals in principle but not signing them yet, the Crimean Bridge hit for a third time in four days, Cyclone Akvan landing in Odisha, the COP31 draft dropping all fossil-fuel language, the California probe into Nvidia, Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth, and a Houthi missile damaging Riyadh's PIF Tower. Every story was reported on the day itself and is drawn from wire and agency reporting — sources: Reuters, AP, BBC, CNN, The Times, NASA and others — and each story was confirmed against two independent sources before it earned its place on the page.

## How it works

The page itself (`entry.png`, 1024×1024) was composed programmatically with PIL. The entry program, `main.py`, simply publishes it: it checks that the bundled image is present and is a PNG, then copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, creating `/output` if needed, and verifies the copy matches the original by size and sha256. Running it repeatedly produces a byte-identical output. Python 3.13, standard library only, no network access.
